CEO Design System · v1.0 · Riyadh — الرياض

Executive presence, measured

A dashboard set like a private annual report.

CEO manages the public presence of policymakers, investors, founders and C‑level leaders. Their dashboard should feel like the brand's own world — misty, dark, quiet, confident. Numbers on the page, not in boxes. One teal, rationed. Everything else is ink and air.

01

Palette

Dark is the primary mode. The ground is a near‑black to teal‑black gradient; surfaces appear only where a panel is genuinely needed. The emerald‑teal accent is reserved for data, deltas and active states — it never decorates.

Ration the teal. On any screen the accent should touch fewer than ~5% of pixels: the chart line, the delta, the active nav tick. Charts are single‑colour — teal data on the dark ground, with the muted gray reserved for a previous‑period comparison (always paired with a legend and direct labels, never colour alone). A light variant exists for print/export: pale gray ground #F7F9F8, near‑black ink, a single teal gradient corner, accent deepened to #12907F for contrast. 03

The bar‑stroke motif

The logo's ≡ symbol — three horizontal strokes — is the system's only ornament. It recurs quietly: as the mark itself, as loading skeletons, as axis ticks, as the line vocabulary of every icon. Nothing else is drawn.

04

05

Discipline

The system is defined as much by what it refuses. If a screen feels sparse, that is correct — it should feel calm and expensive.

Never

  • Multicolour or purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, neon spread everywhere
  • Grids of identical rounded cards; icons in coloured circles
  • Drop shadows — elevation is a soft glow, and only when active
  • Pie charts, rainbow series, chart legends that aren't essential
  • Dense layouts that fill every pixel; emoji or illustration packs
  • Bouncy easing, spinners — loading is the ≡ skeleton

Always

  • Numbers directly on the ground — large, thin, tabular
  • Hairlines before borders; borders before panels
  • One accent, rationed to data and active states
  • Asymmetric layout: a wide main column, a narrow secondary one
  • Motion at 240–420ms, ease‑out, fade and glow only
  • Copy in the concierge voice — composed, specific, never salesy
